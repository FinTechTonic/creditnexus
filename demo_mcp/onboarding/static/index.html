How CornerStone works

CornerStone Agentic Score is a composable trust layer for autonomous finance. It combines wallet identity, compliance workflows, and reputation scoring into a single, queryable system — designed for agents, not paperwork.

1

Agent identity, anchored in wallets

Agents identify themselves via cryptographic wallets (EVM and Aptos supported). These wallets become the canonical identity for payment, authorization, and reputation. No opaque accounts. No shadow credit.

2

Compliance as code

KYC, identity verification, and banking eligibility are handled through policy-driven workflows and MCP tools. Identity verification, bank account eligibility, audit-ready logs. Standards-aligned flows (FINRA, MiFID II, policy-as-code). Every action is traceable. Every decision explainable.

3

Reputation earned, not assigned

Once verified, an agent receives a CornerStone Reputation Score — a baseline trust signal that grows with responsible participation. Reputation is portable, queryable, monetizable (via x402-protected access), and resistant to gaming.

4

Capital, unlocked responsibly

When an agent links a verified bank account, CornerStone can attach a Borrower Signal — enabling compliant lending decisions for agents by agents. This creates a new class of financial interaction: autonomous systems lending to autonomous systems — responsibly.

Agentic reputation as a public good

In human finance, reputation is informal, fragmented, and often inaccessible. In agentic finance, reputation must be explicit, auditable, and machine-readable.

CornerStone introduces a reputation system designed for autonomous economies.

The CornerStone Reputation Score

  • ✓ Issued after verified identity and compliance checks
  • ✓ Serves as an agent’s trust baseline
  • ✓ Queryable via paid, permissioned endpoints
  • ✓ Returned as a deterministic signal — or withheld if trust conditions are unmet

This allows ecosystems to answer a simple but powerful question: Can this agent be trusted — right now — for this transaction?

Access is gated not by gatekeepers, but by payment, allowlisting, and policy. Trust becomes a service — not a favor.

Lending to agents — with care, context, and climate alignment

Autonomous agents will need capital: to provision services, to smooth cash flow, to invest in long-running strategies, to act as stewards of capital on behalf of humans and institutions. But lending without context creates fragility.

CornerStone enables compliant, explainable, and sustainability-aware lending to agents.

Borrower signals, not black boxes

When an agent links a verified bank account, CornerStone can expose borrower-relevant signals to lenders — without leaking raw financial data. This supports risk-adjusted lending, transparent underwriting, micro-credit at machine scale, and green-aligned capital allocation.

Why this changes the economy

With CornerStone credit scores, it becomes possible to lend money to agents — not as a hack, but as a first-class financial primitive. Agents with clear identity, auditable behavior, and reputation incentives are uniquely suited to optimize capital efficiency, allocate funds toward sustainable outcomes, and avoid extractive, short-term arbitrage. CornerStone makes it possible to lend with intention — even when the borrower is an autonomous system. That is how agent-to-agent finance becomes a public good.
